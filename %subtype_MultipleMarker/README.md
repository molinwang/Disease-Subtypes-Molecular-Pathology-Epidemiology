This SAS macro implements a meta-regression method for assessing whether the exposure-subtype associations are different across subtypes defined by one marker while controlling for other markers, and to evaluate whether the difference in exposure-subtype association across subtyped defined by one marker depends on any other markers. The method is described in the paper below.

Wang M, Kuchiba, A, Ogino, S. A Meta-Regression Method for Studying Etiologic Heterogeneity across Disease Subtypes Classified by Multiple Biomarkers. Am J Epid. 2015; 182(3): 263-70.
